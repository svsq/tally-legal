Tally — Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 28, 2026

Tally ("the App") is a currency converter developed by Slava Bilokur ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the App collects, why, and how it's handled. Tally is built to work fully offline and does not require or offer any account or sign-in.

Short version: Tally doesn't collect your name, email, or any personally-identifying information. Exchange rates and your app preferences are cached on your device. Camera frames used for price scanning are processed entirely on-device and never leave your phone. We use Firebase Crashlytics to help us fix crashes.

Data we do not collect

  • No account creation or sign-in — the App has no login system
  • No name, email address, or phone number
  • No precise location — the App only reads your device's region/locale setting to suggest a default currency during first-run setup
  • No photos or video are stored, transmitted, or uploaded — see "Camera (Scan feature)" below
  • We do not sell or share data with data brokers or advertisers

Data stored on your device

The App caches the following locally, using on-device storage. This data is never sent to us and stays on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall it:

  • Cached exchange rates and their last-updated timestamp (so the App works offline)
  • Your selected currencies, display order, and active base currency
  • Any custom rate overrides you set on the Rates screen
  • App preferences — theme, accent color, and whether onboarding has been completed

Network requests (exchange rate data)

To fetch current and historical exchange rates, the App makes requests to public, key-free rate providers:

  • fawazahmed0/exchange-api (served via the jsDelivr CDN and a Cloudflare Pages backup)
  • Frankfurter (ECB-backed rate data), used as a fallback

These requests only ask for public currency/date data (e.g. "USD rates for today") — no personal information is included in or collected from these requests.

Camera (Scan feature)

The Scan screen uses your device's camera to detect prices in the real world and convert them. Text recognition runs fully on-device — Google ML Kit on Android and Apple's Vision framework on iOS — directly on the camera preview. No image, video frame, or recognized text is ever stored, transmitted, or uploaded anywhere; it only exists transiently in memory while the Scan screen is open. Camera access is requested at the OS level and only used while you're actively using the Scan screen.

Diagnostics and crash reporting

The App uses Firebase Crashlytics (a Google service) to automatically collect crash reports and basic technical diagnostics when something goes wrong — for example, a stack trace, device model, OS version, and app version. This data helps us find and fix bugs. It is associated with an anonymous installation identifier, not with your name or any other personally-identifying information.

The App may also use Firebase Analytics to understand, in aggregate, which features are used (e.g. which screens are opened) so we can prioritize improvements. This data is anonymized and is not linked to your identity.

Who we share data with

We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with advertisers or data brokers. The only parties that see any data related to your use of the App are the service providers described above, strictly for the purpose stated:

  • Google (Firebase Crashlytics / Firebase Analytics) — receives anonymized crash diagnostics and, if enabled, anonymized feature-usage data, to help us fix bugs and prioritize improvements
  • fawazahmed0/exchange-api and Frankfurter — receive only the currency codes/dates needed to fetch public exchange rate data; no data about you or your device is sent to these providers

None of these providers receive your name, email address, or any other personally-identifying information from us, because we don't collect it in the first place.

Data security

Data cached locally by the App (rates, preferences, custom rate overrides) is stored in your device's app-sandboxed storage, protected by your device's own OS-level security. Diagnostic data sent to Firebase Crashlytics/Analytics is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored on Google's infrastructure under Google's own security practices for Firebase.

Data retention and deletion

  • On your device: cached rates, preferences, and custom rate overrides remain until you clear the App's storage or uninstall it — entirely under your control, at any time (see "Your choices" below)
  • Crash/diagnostics data (Firebase Crashlytics/Analytics): retained by Google under Firebase's standard retention periods before automatic deletion; we don't keep a separate copy. Because this data isn't tied to your name or any other identifier we hold, there's no "delete my account" step needed — there's no account. If you'd still like to ask about diagnostic data tied to a specific crash report, contact us (see "Contact" below) and we'll help within the limits of what Firebase makes identifiable to us.

Advertising and purchases

At the time of this policy's effective date, the version of the App you're testing does not display ads or offer in-app purchases. A future version is planned to include a bottom-banner ad (via Google AdMob) and an optional one-time "Remove Ads" purchase processed by Google Play's billing system. When that ships, this policy will be updated beforehand to describe AdMob's data practices, and you'll be able to review the updated policy before that version reaches you.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Your choices

  • Clear the App's local cache and preferences any time via your device's App info → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling the App
  • Deny or revoke camera permission at any time in your device settings — the Scan screen simply won't be usable without it; every other feature works normally

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above and, for material changes (such as adding advertising), post the update before the change reaches you.
