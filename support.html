Tally — Support

Questions, bugs or feedback: slavabilokur.support@gmail.com. Please include your device model, OS version and app version.

Where do exchange rates come from?

Tally pulls mid-market reference rates and refreshes them while you have a connection. The last set is cached, so the app keeps working offline. The Convert screen shows when rates were last updated.

Can I set my own rate?

Yes. Open the Rates tab, switch on a pair, and type the rate your bank or cash desk is offering. Your override is used everywhere in the app until you turn it off.

What is free and what is in Tally PRO?

Conversions, charts, custom rates and offline mode are free. The free version keeps up to 5 currencies on the Convert screen and includes 5 price scans. Tally PRO is a one-time purchase that removes both limits.

How do I restore a purchase?

Sign in to the same store account, open the More tab and tap “Restore purchase” on the Tally PRO card.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by the store. On iPhone, request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, use your Google Play order history.

Are the rates financial advice?

No. Rates are for reference only and can differ from what a provider charges. Confirm with your bank or exchange before acting on an amount.
